STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS Switching Between Cable TV & Netflix
1

Pick up the Samsung TV Remote (the smaller black remote with the BLUE tape on the bottom).

2

Press the Home button in the middle (looks like a little house ) to bring up the bottom menu bar.

3

Use the Left / Right circle ring arrows to highlight Netflix or YouTube, then press the center OK button.

4

To go back to regular Cable TV: Press the House button again, scroll left to "Bell TV (HDMI 1)", and press OK.

5

Once Bell TV is back on screen, set down the Samsung remote and use your Bell Remote (RED tape) for channels.

6

If the screen ever says "No Signal", press the Power button on the RED-taped Bell remote to wake up the cable box.

Important Scam Safety Golden Rules

  • Microsoft, Apple, Bell, and TD Bank will NEVER call you or pop up on screen asking for remote access or gift cards.
  • If a loud warning ever locks up your computer screen, hold the physical power button for 5 seconds to turn it off, then call Mike!
  • Turned on 2-Step Verification (2FA) on your Gmail so nobody can log in without texting your cell phone first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do you charge, and what is included?

To keep things simple and completely transparent, we offer two straightforward options:

  • On-Site In-Person Support ($100 CAD per hour): Includes hands-on support in your home or local meeting spot, customized written instructions left behind for you, a backup copy of your notes kept on file, and two free 10-minute follow-up phone calls.
  • Phone Support ($40 CAD per half hour): Perfect for smaller questions, guided step-by-step walkthroughs, or quick troubleshooting over the phone when a full home visit isn't needed.

What areas do you serve?

For on-site appointments, we proudly serve Barrie, Ontario and the surrounding areas! We come directly to your home, business, retirement residence, or can meet you at a local library or coffee shop. For Phone Support, we can help you anywhere!

Can our organization hire you for a group event or workshop?

Yes! Through our Senior Scrollers program, we partner with libraries, retirement homes, churches, and community centres to run educational tech workshops, scam-prevention talks, and drop-in help desks. Reach out via phone or our contact form to discuss your event!

Will you make me feel silly for asking questions?

Absolutely not. Technology changes incredibly fast, and it is perfectly normal to find it confusing. Our core promise is patience, understanding, and absolutely zero judgment.
