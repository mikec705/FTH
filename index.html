About Us

Hi, I'm Mike, the face behind For Tech Help. I started this service in Barrie, ON because I saw how frustrating modern technology can be. My goal is to make technology work for you, not the other way around.

I specialize in helping everyone gain confidence with their devices. Whether you need a persistent virus removed, data transferred to a new phone, a home network configured, or just patient, step-by-step instructions on how to use email, I am here to help. No rushed appointments, no confusing tech jargon—just friendly, understandable support across Barrie and the surrounding areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do you charge, and what is included?

To keep things simple and completely transparent, I charge a flat rate of $100 CAD per hour for individual appointments. This includes hands-on support, customized written instructions left behind for you, a backup copy of your notes kept on file, and two free 10-minute follow-up phone calls so you never feel left on your own.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Barrie, Ontario and the surrounding areas! We come directly to your home, business, retirement residence, or can meet you at a local library or coffee shop.

Can our organization hire you for a group event or workshop?

Yes! Through our Senior Scrollers program, we partner with libraries, retirement homes, churches, and community centres to run educational tech workshops, scam-prevention talks, and drop-in help desks. Reach out via phone or our contact form to discuss your event!

Will you make me feel silly for asking questions?

Absolutely not. Technology changes incredibly fast, and it is perfectly normal to find it confusing. Our core promise is patience, understanding, and absolutely zero judgment.
